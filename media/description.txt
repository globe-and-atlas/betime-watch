A little BeOS-style desktop for your Pebble. BeTime puts the clock in a beveled window with a yellow tab and uses actual Haiku and Haiku Calendar icons.

• Power shows watch battery percentage.
• Today shows the date, with five display options including Off.
• Clock shows seconds.
• Choose Classic Blue, Midnight, Teal, Plum, Graphite, or Monochrome Desktop.
• Customize the desktop, window, text, and tab colors.
• Use the watch preference, 12-hour time, or 24-hour time.

Designed for Pebble Time 2 and Pebble Round 2. Configure BeTime in the Pebble app. Time and battery work offline.

Icon artwork from Haiku and Haiku Calendar is used under MIT licenses, with notices included in the watchface bundle.
